Autonomy Business Capability Specification

ABCS is an open, vendor-neutral specification for describing business capabilities so humans, agents, applications, and automation platforms can discover and execute business operations consistently.

Read the specificationExplore Procurement
Why ABCS

A common language for business capability

Business intent should not be tied to a particular application vendor. ABCS defines stable, application-neutral capability identities that implementations can map to any suitable system.

Vendor-neutral

Describe what a business capability means without embedding SAP, Oracle, Salesforce, ServiceNow, or any other application into the definition.

Agent-ready

Use predictable identifiers, contracts, relationships, and semantics that agents and automation runtimes can discover programmatically.

Implementation-independent

A single canonical capability can have multiple implementations across applications, environments, protocols, and technologies.

Canonical identity

Simple, stable capability identifiers

ABCS Draft 0.1 uses a domain.resource.operation convention. Definitions carry version metadata while canonical identifiers remain stable across compatible revisions.

procurement.purchase-order.get
procurement.purchase-order.search
procurement.purchase-order.create
procurement.purchase-order.approve
procurement.receipt.create
procurement.supplier.get
Architecture

Specification first. Implementations anywhere.

Business definition

ABCS describes domains, resources, capabilities, contracts, processes, policies, events, and relationships in application-neutral terms.

Reference implementation

Sirvisetti Autonomy implements ABCS through governed Processes, Actions, Policies, Adapters, Packs, Agent and Chatbot experiences. Other implementations are welcome.

Initial domain

Procurement proof set

Draft 0.1 intentionally starts small. The initial Procurement domain validates the naming model, contracts, relationships, and implementation mapping before ABCS expands into additional business domains.
