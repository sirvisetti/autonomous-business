A common language for business capability.

The Autonomous Business Capability Specification (ABCS) is an open, vendor-neutral specification created and open-sourced by Sirvisetti for describing what businesses can do, the canonical business information those capabilities use, and the meaning of their outcomes—independent of applications and implementation technology.

Core model

Capability is the universal primitive

Procurement, Finance, Sales, HR and every other ABCS area use the same primitive: a canonical business Capability. Domains classify meaning; they do not create different capability types.

Canonical capability

Stable identifiers such as procurement.purchase-order.submit describe the business action without naming the software that performs it.

Canonical business objects

Purchase Orders, Payments, Customers and other business objects have application-neutral fields, business keys, relationships and state.

Implementation independence

The same capability can be implemented by SAP, Oracle, custom software, humans, workflows, autonomous agents or future technology without changing its ABCS meaning.

Hard boundary

Business meaning, not technology

Would this capability still make business sense if humans performed the work manually and all implementation technology disappeared?

If the answer is no, it does not belong in ABCS. Integration technology, automation engines, schedulers, middleware, transports, authentication, databases and vendor APIs remain implementation concerns.

Business interoperability

One endpoint. Every business capability.

ABCS defines a small transport-neutral invocation envelope plus Discover, Describe and Invoke semantics. An HTTP implementation can choose a single capability endpoint while the ABCS business meaning stays independent of HTTP, middleware and security technology.

POST /capabilities

{
  "abcs": "0.2",
  "capability": "procurement.purchase-order.submit",
  "requestId": "...",
  "from": { "id": "urn:example:walmart" },
  "to":   { "id": "urn:example:walgreens" },
  "payload": { ...canonical purchase order... }
}

Reference scenario

Business meaning survives application boundaries

A buyer can submit the canonical capability and Purchase Order without needing to know whether the receiving business runs SAP, Oracle or custom software.

Walmart
   │
   │  procurement.purchase-order.submit
   │  canonical ABCS Purchase Order
   ▼
Walgreens capability service
   │
   ├── SAP implementation
   ├── Oracle implementation
   └── custom implementation

Open and implementation-neutral

ABCS is not presented as an industry standard or a widely adopted standard. Draft 0.2 is an open specification intended for practical implementation, feedback and evolution. Sirvisetti Autonomy is one reference/commercial implementation; independent implementations are welcome.
