Business domains

Domains are namespaces for enduring business meaning. They are not different capability types, organizational charts, software modules or implementation technologies.

Draft 0.2 now has three levels of domain depth: Procurement is the reference domain; Finance, Sales and Human Resources are modeled domains with canonical Business Objects and representative detailed capability definitions; Service, Logistics, Manufacturing and Projects remain initial catalogs.

What is deliberately absent

There is no Integration or Automation business domain. Those describe how work may be implemented rather than what enduring business meaning the work has. Generic technical IT operations are also outside the ABCS catalog.

New domains must pass the business-only scope test: the capability should retain clear business meaning if humans performed the work manually and all implementation technology disappeared.
