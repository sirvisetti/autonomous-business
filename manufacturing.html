Manufacturing

Manufacturing defines canonical business meaning for production authorization, executable work and material consumption without depending on MES, ERP, shop-floor protocols or machine-control technology.

Production Order

A business authorization to produce a specified item or product quantity within defined planning and production conditions.

Work Order

An executable unit of manufacturing work that may fulfill all or part of a Production Order.

Material Issue

A record that material was issued or consumed for manufacturing work.

Open semantic reference

Draft 0.2 cross-checks manufacturing terminology against Eclipse Tractus-X semantic models for manufacturing capability, parts, bills of material and production-related information. ABCS retains independent, concise business definitions and records the CC BY 4.0 reference in provenance.

Business-only boundary: PLC commands, machine protocols, MES APIs, sensor telemetry, scheduling engines and shop-floor middleware remain outside ABCS.
