Modeled domain

Service

Service defines canonical business meaning for customer cases and service requests without assuming a CRM, ticketing product, contact-center platform or technical service-management system.

Customer Case

A tracked business matter raised by or on behalf of a customer that requires investigation, response or resolution.

service.customer-case.create
service.customer-case.assign
service.customer-case.escalate
service.customer-case.resolve

Service Request

A request by a party for a defined business service to be provided, fulfilled or completed.

service.service-request.get
service.service-request.create
service.service-request.assign
service.service-request.complete

Business state

Customer Cases distinguish lifecycle from assignment. Service Requests distinguish request lifecycle from assignment so a request can be submitted, accepted, in progress and completed without encoding product-specific ticket statuses.

Business-only boundary: ticketing APIs, routing queues, chatbot channels, contact-center infrastructure and technical incident-management mechanics remain outside ABCS.
