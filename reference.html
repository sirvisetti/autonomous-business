Non-normative implementation

From canonical operation to governed business outcome.

The Autonomous Business Reference is intentionally separate from the standard. It demonstrates the governed side of the autonomous-business boundary with a deliberately small implementation: canonical business operations, business state, representative guardrails and explicit outcomes.

Normative boundary: the reference depends on the standard. The standard never depends on the reference. Current conformance is defined by ABCS Draft 0.2 and its canonical contracts; the broader business-governance model is the direction for the next normative draft.

Any requester

An autonomous agent, workflow, application or person may arrive at the same canonical business operation. The reference does not model requester-specific technology.

Representative governance

The Playground demonstrates state checks and business rules before purchase orders, journal entries and leave requests can produce effects.

Explicit outcome

Completed and rejected requests return visible business results and state transitions rather than hiding the consequence behind an implementation API.

Representative operations

procurement.purchase-order.submit
finance.journal-entry.post
hr.leave-request.submit

What the reference does not do

ABR does not simulate natural-language reasoning, agent planning, identity infrastructure, policy engines, workflow runtimes or ERP integration. It begins at the canonical business-operation boundary so the governance concept remains easy to inspect.
