# ABCS Governance

ABCS is created and maintained by Sirvisetti as an open specification. Draft 0.x releases are pre-1.0 and may evolve as the model is tested across additional business domains and independent implementations.

Sirvisetti acts as the initial specification maintainer. Public feedback and contributions are welcome. The project intends to preserve stable canonical identifiers and backward-compatible evolution once ABCS reaches 1.0.
