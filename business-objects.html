Canonical semantics

Business Objects

A Business Object defines canonical business information used by capabilities. It is application-neutral and is not an execution primitive.

Business keys, not artificial global IDs

ABCS distinguishes the identity of an object type from the identity of a business record. An individual record may be identified by a business number scoped to a relevant business participant or organization. ABCS does not require every enterprise record to acquire an artificial global UUID.

Party and contextual roles

A Party may be an organization or person. Supplier, Customer, Buyer, Seller, Payer, Payee and Carrier are contextual business roles. sales.customer and procurement.supplier therefore preserve domain meaning while sharing reusable Party identity semantics.

Multidimensional state

Complex objects do not collapse independent dimensions into one giant status. Purchase Orders distinguish lifecycle, approval, fulfillment and invoicing; Invoices distinguish lifecycle, approval and settlement; Sales Orders distinguish lifecycle, fulfillment and invoicing.

Closed canonical schemas

Draft 0.2 canonical schemas remain deliberately strict. Vendor-specific application fields, routing metadata and implementation details do not belong in canonical payloads.

ABCS object schemas describe business semantics. SAP fields, Oracle row IDs, adapter IDs, environment IDs, API endpoints, scheduler metadata and similar implementation data remain outside the canonical object.
