Finance

Finance defines canonical business meaning for invoices, payments, journal entries and payment runs. Draft 0.2 models these objects deeply enough to establish identifiers, relationships, state and representative capability contracts while remaining application-neutral.

Invoice

A financial document issued by one party to another stating amounts due. Canonical state distinguishes lifecycle, approval and settlement.

Payment

A transfer of monetary value from a payer to a payee, optionally related to one or more invoices.

Journal Entry

A balanced accounting record containing debit and credit lines for a business transaction or adjustment.

Payment Run

A business grouping of payments selected, reviewed, scheduled and executed together.

finance.payment-run.create
finance.payment-run.schedule
finance.payment-run.approve

Business scheduling, not scheduler infrastructure

finance.payment-run.schedule is valid ABCS business meaning because a Payment Run is itself a business object. Cron expressions, batch schedulers, queues, jobs and runtime timing mechanics remain outside ABCS.

Cross-domain relationships

An Invoice may reference a Procurement Purchase Order or Sales Order. Those relationships preserve business meaning without making Finance dependent on any ERP module or application vendor.

Business-only boundary: bank protocols, payment rails, ERP posting APIs, middleware mappings, authentication and transport are implementation concerns. ABCS defines the business objects and business actions.
