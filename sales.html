Modeled domain

Sales

Sales defines canonical business meaning for Customers, Quotes, Sales Orders and Returns. The model reuses Party identity while preserving domain-specific customer semantics.

Customer

A Party viewed in the business role of purchasing or receiving goods or services from a seller.

Customer is a role

A Customer is not a separate universal identity from a Supplier or other participant. ABCS reuses Party identity and models Customer as a Sales-domain business role, allowing the same organization to act as customer, supplier, buyer, seller, payer or payee in different contexts.

Independent state dimensions

Sales Orders distinguish lifecycle, fulfillment and invoicing. A Sales Order can therefore be open, partially fulfilled and partially invoiced at the same time without inventing a compound status code.

Business-only boundary: CRM APIs, storefront protocols, middleware, order-routing technology and application-specific status codes remain outside ABCS.
