Governance

ABCS is created and maintained by Sirvisetti as an open specification. The project is intended to evolve through practical implementation experience and public feedback.

Current status

Draft 0.1 is pre-1.0. Definitions may change as the model is validated across additional business domains and implementations.

Contribution principles

  • Canonical capabilities must be vendor-neutral and business-oriented.
  • Definitions should be concise enough for humans and precise enough for machines.
  • New capabilities should avoid duplicating existing semantics.
  • External source material must be compatible with the project's GREEN-only provenance policy.
  • Application-specific behavior belongs in implementations, not canonical capability definitions.

GREEN-only provenance

ABCS does not incorporate proprietary process taxonomies, paid process libraries, or ambiguously licensed business definitions. Contributions must be original or based only on material whose terms clearly permit the intended use and redistribution.

License

ABCS specification source is made available under the Apache License 2.0. The Sirvisetti and Autonomy names, logos, and other brand assets remain trademarks/brand assets of Sirvisetti and are not granted for unrestricted use by the specification license.

Road to 1.0

The project will remain in Draft 0.x while the capability model, identifiers, schemas, compatibility rules, and domain coverage are validated. ABCS 1.0 will mark the first stable compatibility commitment.

Contribute

Public issues and pull requests will be accepted through the sirvisetti/autonomy-capability repository after publication.
