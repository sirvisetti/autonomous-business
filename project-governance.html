Project governance & contributions

The Autonomous Business Standard is created and stewarded by Sirvisetti and developed in the open. This page covers how the standards project is maintained. It is separate from business-operation governance, which is part of the standard's emerging autonomous-business model.

Business-only scope gate

Would the proposed capability or governance rule still make business sense if humans performed the work manually and all implementation technology disappeared?

Every proposed domain, capability and business-governance concept must pass this test. Middleware, integration infrastructure, generic automation, transports, schedulers and vendor APIs are rejected from the canonical business model even when they are useful implementation technologies.

Domain maturity

  • Reference — modeled in depth and used to demonstrate the full pattern.
  • Modeled — canonical objects, schemas, keys/state/relationships and representative capability definitions are present.
  • Initial — vocabulary/catalog only.

Maturity labels describe specification completeness, not adoption.

GREEN provenance policy

Canonical content must be original work or use clearly open sources whose terms permit the intended use. Proprietary or licensing-ambiguous frameworks are excluded from canonical source material.

Reviewed sources and their disposition are recorded in PROVENANCE.md. Open references are used selectively for semantic cross-checking rather than copied wholesale.

Representations

The specification maintains one logical model. YAML 1.2 is the human-authoring representation for modular definitions, JSON is the preferred machine publication/interchange representation, and JSON Schema 2020-12 is the normative validation language. See FORMATS.md.

Compatibility discipline

Published identifiers retain their established business meaning. Compatible revisions may add optional information. Removing required information, changing canonical field meaning or changing defined business effects is breaking and requires explicit version treatment.

Positioning

ABS is an emerging open standard project and ABCS is an open normative specification under development. Neither should be described as an established industry standard, standards-body-approved standard or widely adopted standard unless future evidence supports that claim.
